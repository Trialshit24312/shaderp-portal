Join the server

Login with Discord, join the queue, then connect to FiveM when your slot is ready — same flow as LiquidRP.

How it works

  1. Login — Use Discord so we can match your FiveM account.
  2. Join queue — Click Join Queue and keep this tab open (or check back).
  3. Wait for ready — You'll see your position and get a sound when it's your turn.
  4. Connect — Click Connect to FiveM or use the cfx.re link on the Connect page.

Discord must be linked in FiveM (Settings → Linked accounts). Staff can use Priority Queue.
